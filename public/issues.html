How Scoring & Ward Metrics Work

CivicWatch uses a 100% transparent, rule-based algorithm. Scores are not generated by black-box AI; every point is deterministically calculated from verifiable civic actions.

1. Resolution Score (0–100 Points)

  • Verification Quality (Max 40 pts): +40 for certified officer Before/After verification; +25 for submitted work; +10 for legacy direct resolution.
  • SLA Timeliness (Max 30 pts): Based on severity SLA (Open Manhole: 2 days, Drainage: 3 days, Pothole/Water: 5 days, Streetlight/Garbage: 7 days). Resolved within SLA = +30 pts; $\le 2\times$ SLA = +20 pts.
  • Evidence Completeness (Max 30 pts): +15 for official Firebase Storage photo; +10 for work description $\ge 20$ chars; +5 for citizen before photo comparison.
  • Penalties: -15 for rework rejection; -20 if reopened after resolution.

2. Ward Performance Score (0–100 Points)

Wards are evaluated using weighted accountability metrics:

Ward Score = (30% × Resolution Rate) + (20% × Timeliness Score) + (20% × Avg Resolution Score) + (10% × High Priority Rate) + (10% × Evidence Rate) + (10% × Reopen Discipline)

Sample Size Notice: Wards with fewer than 3 complaints display a "Low Sample Size" badge to prevent statistical distortion.
